# LeetCode Track

**Target:** Hard fluency on Trees, Graphs, DP — required bar for Google and Meta.  
**Source:** [NeetCode 150](https://neetcode.io/practice) — work in pattern order.  
**Problem:** You can solve problems but slowly. Fix: drill patterns for speed, not just correctness.

---

## Two Phases

### Weeks 1–8: Medium Speed (5 problems/week)
Build pattern recognition and speed on Medium. You can solve these — the goal is fluency, not understanding. Time yourself: 15 min per Easy, 25 min per Medium. If you go over, note it and move on.

### Weeks 9–16: Hard Ramp (3 Medium + 2 Hard per week)
Add Hard problems on the patterns that matter most for big tech: Trees, Graphs, DP. Don't skip Mediums — they keep your speed up. The Hard problems are where Google/Meta filter.

### Weeks 17–20: Timed Mocks
Full 45-minute mock sessions. Mix of Medium and Hard. Do these on paper or a whiteboard when possible — it more closely mirrors the interview format. Review solutions only after the timer.

## Weekly Practice Format

1. **Attempt first** — no hints, set a timer (15 min Easy, 25 min Medium, 45 min Hard)
2. **If stuck at timer:** note where you got stuck, look at the pattern hint only (not full solution)
3. **After solving or reading solution:** understand *why* this pattern applies, not just *what* the code does
4. **Re-implement from memory** the same day — this is what cements it
5. **Log one line per problem** in your weekly log

## High-Priority Problems for AI/ML Engineer Interviews

These come up most often — prioritize if time is short:

- **Top K Frequent Elements** (Heap + bucket sort)
- **Word Break** (DP + trie)
- **Course Schedule** (topological sort — relevant for dependency graphs, agent DAGs)
- **LRU Cache** (design + linked list)
- **Serialize/Deserialize Binary Tree** (recursion + design)
- **Longest Common Subsequence** (DP — edit distance variant)
- **Find Median from Data Stream** (two heaps)
- **Alien Dictionary** (topological sort — graph)
- **Sliding Window Maximum** (monotonic deque)
- **Word Ladder** (BFS — shortest path)
